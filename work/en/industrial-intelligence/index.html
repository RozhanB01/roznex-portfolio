Industrial Intelligence

An industrial experience designed to grow beyond presentation into operational data, management workflows and AI.

In developmentROZNEX IndustrialIndustrial platform2026

An industrial project is not simply a company website. Once factory storytelling, 3D, operational data and AI share the same system, the architecture has to be designed for growth from the beginning.

Context

Industrial Intelligence is a flagship ROZNEX project in development, combining premium industrial presentation with future-ready layers for operational data, management workflows, AI agents and immersive 3D.

FOCUS3D + AI + Operations
STATUSIn development
DESIGN GOALIndustrial clarity
ARCHITECTUREScalable modules

Challenge

An industrial website often has two jobs: communicate credibility to external buyers and partners, while remaining capable of accepting future data, dashboards, requests and operational tools without a full rebuild.

System

  • A presentation layer for the facility, projects and equipment.
  • Multilingual B2B content architecture.
  • Management and data modules that can be added progressively.
  • A controlled place for AI-agent automation.
  • A reduced 3D experience for mobile and lower-powered devices.

Design decisions

3D must explain

The scene needs to improve understanding of the facility, product or process and respect a performance budget.

Modular architecture

The website, CMS, dashboards and AI layer are treated as separate but connectable systems.

Mobile fallback

Mobile is deliberately composed so 3D never overrides readability or speed.

Status

The project is still in development. This case study therefore documents the problem and architecture rather than claiming outcomes that have not yet been measured.
