LILIUM Store — Ecommerce

A Persian ecommerce experience combining marketplace structure, a recognizable visual identity and responsive RTL design.

PublishedLILIUME-commerce / UI/UX2022

LILIUM Store is a Persian ecommerce concept combining marketplace structure, RTL interface design and a responsive shopping flow.

Context

LILIUM is a Persian ecommerce site with search, categories, special offers, best sellers, brands and a shopping cart. Its visual identity uses a cream and lime palette.

CLIENTLILIUM
CATEGORYE-commerce / UI/UX
LANGUAGEPersian / RTL
STATUSPublished demo

Challenge

Persian ecommerce interfaces carry dense information: product, price, category, offers and CTAs compete for attention. The design needs a strong hierarchy that still works on smaller screens.

System

  • Marketplace-style product and category structure.
  • Search, promotions, best sellers and brands.
  • Shopping-cart and purchase-flow concepts.
  • Responsive RTL UI across mobile and desktop.

Design decisions

Hierarchy before decoration

Product information needs to remain scannable before visual accents are added.

Mobile shopping flow

Product cards and CTAs must stay legible and usable at narrow widths.

Recognizable visual identity

The cream and lime palette creates character without reducing product readability.
