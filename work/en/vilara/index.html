Vilara — Travel Website

A modern travel discovery experience focused on destinations, accommodation, visual trust and responsive use.

PublishedVilaraWeb design2023

Vilara is built for travel discovery, where imagery, trust and the path to destinations or stays need to work together.

Context

Vilara is a Persian travel website focused on destinations and accommodation. The goal was a simple, modern and trustworthy experience across mobile and desktop.

CLIENTVilara
CATEGORYWeb design
LANGUAGEPersian / RTL
STATUSPublished demo

Challenge

Travel content can easily become visually crowded. The structure needed to let imagery lead while keeping selection and trust signals easy to understand.

System

  • Visual structure for destinations and accommodation.
  • RTL interface and Persian typography.
  • Responsive behavior across mobile and desktop.
  • A simple path for discovering and choosing travel services.

Design decisions

Imagery as content

Photography is part of the decision, not just decoration.

True RTL composition

The layout is designed for right-to-left use rather than simply reversing text.

Lower friction

The page structure aims to move people toward useful options without unnecessary cognitive load.
